# Business Recommendations

## Overview

The following recommendations are derived directly from the analytical findings obtained from the Olist dataset.

---

## 1. Strengthen Weekend Marketing Activities

### Finding

Overall sales performance decreases during weekends.

### Recommendation

Launch targeted promotions focused on categories that already demonstrate strong weekend demand, including:

* Fixed Telephony
* Health & Beauty
* Computer Accessories
* Garden Tools
* Watches & Gifts

### Expected Impact

Increase weekend revenue while focusing marketing spend on categories with proven customer interest.

---

## 2. Leverage High-Performing Weekdays

### Finding

Monday and Tuesday consistently generate stronger sales performance than weekends.

### Recommendation

Schedule key promotional campaigns and product launches at the beginning of the week.

### Expected Impact

Maximize campaign effectiveness during periods of higher customer purchasing activity.

---

## 3. Prioritize High-Revenue Categories

### Finding

A limited number of categories generate the majority of total revenue.

### Recommendation

Allocate additional marketing resources and inventory planning efforts to top-performing categories.

### Expected Impact

Improved return on marketing investment and stronger revenue growth.

---

## 4. Optimize Product Portfolio

### Finding

Product-level revenue distribution is highly concentrated.

Some products generate substantial revenue while others generate very little.

### Recommendation

Regularly review low-performing products and evaluate:

* Product replacement opportunities
* Product repositioning
* Promotional support

### Expected Impact

Improved inventory efficiency and reduced carrying costs.

---

## 5. Implement Bundle Strategies

### Finding

Several categories contain both high-performing and low-performing products.

### Recommendation

Bundle top-selling products with low-performing products from the same category.

### Expected Impact

* Improved visibility of low-performing products
* Higher average order value
* Increased inventory turnover

---

## Summary

The most impactful opportunities identified in this analysis are:

1. Weekend sales optimization
2. Early-week campaign prioritization
3. Product bundling strategies
4. Category-focused resource allocation
5. Product portfolio optimization

These actions are directly supported by observed sales patterns within the dataset.
