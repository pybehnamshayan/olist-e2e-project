# Weekday vs Weekend Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate differences in customer purchasing behavior between weekdays and weekends.

---

## Key Findings

### Strong Weekday Performance

Multiple products generated significantly higher revenue during Monday and Tuesday than during weekends.

In some cases, the revenue difference exceeded 70,000 monetary units.

---

### Weekend Demand Persistence

Despite lower overall sales activity, several categories remained strong performers during weekends:

* Fixed Telephony
* Health & Beauty
* Computer Accessories
* Garden Tools
* Watches & Gifts

---

### Customer Behavior Pattern

The findings suggest that:

* Customers exhibit stronger purchasing activity early in the week.
* Weekend demand is concentrated in specific product categories.

---

## Business Implications

Marketing campaigns should be adapted to reflect weekly purchasing behavior.

A differentiated strategy for weekdays and weekends may improve conversion performance and marketing efficiency.
