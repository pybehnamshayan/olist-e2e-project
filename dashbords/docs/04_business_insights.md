# Business Insights

## Overview

This document summarizes the key business insights derived from the Olist e-commerce sales dataset.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, product categories, customer purchasing patterns across weekdays and weekends, and product-level revenue concentration.

---

## Sales Concentration by Product Category

Revenue is highly concentrated in a small number of product categories.

Top revenue-generating categories include:

| Category             | Revenue |
| -------------------- | ------: |
| Bed, Bath & Table    |   3.43M |
| Health & Beauty      |   3.31M |
| Computer Accessories |   3.17M |
| Home Decoration      |   2.86M |
| Watches & Gifts      |   2.86M |

These categories represent the primary revenue drivers of the business and should be monitored closely.

---

## Product Volume vs Revenue

The categories generating the highest revenue are not always the categories with the highest sales volume.

For example:

* Watches & Gifts ranks among the highest revenue categories.
* However, it does not appear among the top volume categories.

This indicates that some categories generate higher revenue per unit sold and may contain higher-value products.

---

## Weekly Purchasing Behavior

Sales activity follows a clear weekly pattern.

Analysis shows that:

* Monday and Tuesday generate the strongest sales performance.
* Weekend sales are significantly lower.
* Several products demonstrate strong weekday preference with substantial revenue gaps between weekdays and weekends.

This indicates that customer purchasing behavior changes throughout the week.

---

## Weekend Demand Opportunities

Although overall weekend sales are lower, some categories continue to perform strongly:

* Fixed Telephony
* Health & Beauty
* Computer Accessories
* Garden Tools
* Watches & Gifts

These categories demonstrate resilient demand and represent strong candidates for weekend-focused marketing campaigns.

---

## Revenue Concentration at Product Level

Revenue is heavily concentrated in a limited number of products.

Top-performing products generated revenues exceeding 100,000 monetary units, while some products generated less than 50.

This significant disparity highlights opportunities for assortment optimization and targeted merchandising strategies.

---

## Summary

The analysis reveals four major business patterns:

1. Revenue is concentrated in a small number of categories.
2. Customer purchasing behavior differs between weekdays and weekends.
3. Certain categories maintain strong weekend demand despite lower overall sales.
4. Product revenue distribution is highly uneven, creating opportunities for product optimization and bundling initiatives.
