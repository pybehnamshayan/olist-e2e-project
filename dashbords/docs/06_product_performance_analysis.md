# Product Performance Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate product-level sales performance and identify revenue concentration patterns.

---

## Key Findings

### Top Revenue Products

Several products generated exceptionally high revenue, exceeding 100,000 monetary units.

The highest-performing products belong primarily to:

* Fixed Telephony
* Health & Beauty
* Garden Tools
* Computer Accessories
* Home Decoration

---

### Revenue Distribution

Product performance is highly skewed.

Examples from the dataset show:

* Top products generated more than 200,000 in revenue.
* Some products generated less than 25.

This indicates a significant concentration of sales among a relatively small group of products.

---

### Business Implications

Revenue concentration suggests that:

* Top products should receive increased visibility.
* Product availability should be monitored carefully.
* Product-level performance tracking is essential for merchandising decisions.

---

## Conclusion

A small percentage of products drive a disproportionate share of total revenue, creating opportunities for focused product management and promotional strategies.
