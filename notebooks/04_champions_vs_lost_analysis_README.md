## Objective

The goal of this analysis is to quantify the financial impact of customer churn by comparing:

* Champion Customers
* Lost High-Value Customers

Instead of measuring churn only as customer count, this analysis focuses on lost revenue opportunity.

---

## Methodology

The analysis follows these steps:

1. Build RFM Segmentation
2. Identify Champion Customers
3. Identify Lost Customers
4. Detect Lost High-Value Customers
5. Compare category-level revenue
6. Estimate unrealized revenue opportunity

## Strategic Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

* Launch win-back campaigns for lost high-value customers.
* Investigate root causes of churn in high-risk categories.
* Replicate retention strategies from best-performing categories.

### Long-Term Actions

* Build churn prediction models.
* Develop customer lifetime value models.
* Create personalized retention campaigns.
* Integrate churn monitoring into Power BI dashboards.
