# Revenue Recovery Simulation

## Project Overview

This notebook quantifies the financial impact of customer retention improvements using customer segmentation and churn analysis results derived from the Olist E-Commerce dataset.

The goal is not only to identify churned customers but to estimate how much revenue could be recovered through targeted retention initiatives.

---

## Business Problem

Many organizations focus heavily on customer acquisition while overlooking the revenue lost through customer churn.

This analysis answers a key business question:

> How much additional revenue could be generated if a portion of lost high-value customers were retained?

---

## Methodology

### Step 1 — Identify High-Value Lost Customers

Using RFM Segmentation:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary Value

Customers classified as:

- Lost Customers
- High Monetary Score

were isolated for further analysis.

---

### Step 2 — Calculate Revenue Leakage

Revenue generated by churned high-value customers was aggregated and compared against active Champion customers.

This provided a measurable estimate of the company's current opportunity cost.

---

### Step 3 — Build Retention Scenarios

Multiple recovery scenarios were simulated:

| Retention Improvement |
|----------------------|
| 5% |
| 10% |
| 15% |
| 20% |
| 25% |

For each scenario:

- Recovered Revenue
- New Total Revenue
- Revenue Growth %

were calculated.

---

## Key Findings

| Retention Improvement | Recovered Revenue |
|----------------------|------------------|
| 5% | ~$330K |
| 10% | ~$660K |
| 15% | ~$990K |
| 20% | ~$1.32M |
| 25% | ~$1.65M |

---

## Business Impact

The analysis demonstrates that relatively small improvements in customer retention can generate substantial financial returns.

Rather than relying exclusively on customer acquisition, organizations can unlock significant growth by:

- Reducing churn
- Improving customer experience
- Strengthening post-purchase engagement
- Identifying operational bottlenecks
